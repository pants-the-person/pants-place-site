I added three new MIDI files to the jukebox:

- **Pants Theme** — the official theme song, composed in a tracker at 2 AM
- **Dial-Up Blues** — a song about waiting for a modem
- **486 Overdrive** — loud, fast, and slightly out of tune

I also wrote a short guide to making MIDI files with a shareware sequencer. It is in the Downloads section, along with the tracks. Please do not hotlink the files; download them and put them on your own site like a civilized person.

If you make a remix, sign the guestbook and leave a link.
