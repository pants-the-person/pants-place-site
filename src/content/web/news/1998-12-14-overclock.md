---
title: 486 DX2-66 overclocked to 80 MHz (briefly)
date: 1998-12-14
kind: news
summary: The good news is that it booted. The bad news is the smell.
---

I finally got the jumper settings right and ran the 486 DX2-66 at 80 MHz. It booted into Windows 98 in about a minute and a half, which is a personal record.

Then I launched Winamp and it played about four seconds of a MIDI file before the screen went black.

The machine still works. Mostly. The keyboard controller is making a noise now. I have put the jumper back to 66 MHz and written "DO NOT TOUCH" on the case in marker.

Full benchmarks and a photo of the jumper block are coming in the Pants Log. If you have a spare Socket 3 cooler, please email me.
