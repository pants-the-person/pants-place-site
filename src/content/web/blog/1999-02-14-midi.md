There is a MIDI file that ships with Windows. You have heard it. It is the one that sounds like a very polite jazz band trapped inside a computer. It plays when you open a certain folder, or maybe when you install something, or maybe it just lives in your head now.

I have been trying to recreate that sound with my own MIDI files. The secret is not the notes. It is the **program change**. The default Windows MIDI synth is a little box of approximations, and if you pick the right instrument, you can make anything sound like 1998.

My new track, "Dial-Up Blues," uses a fretless bass and a very sad saxophone. It is the most emotionally honest thing I have made. It is also 668 bytes.

You can download it from the Downloads page. Play it in Winamp. Turn the volume up. Think about modems.
