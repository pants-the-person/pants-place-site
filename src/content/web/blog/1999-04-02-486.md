title: Why I still use a 486
date: 1999-04-02

summary: It is slower, louder, and more honest than your Pentium III.

My main machine is a 486 DX2-66 with 32 MB of RAM and a 540 MB hard drive. It runs Windows 98, Winamp, and a web browser, which is all I need.

People ask why I do not upgrade. The answer is that the 486 has character. The fan is loud. The case has a turbo button that does nothing. The BIOS makes a little beep when it starts, like a tiny robot saying hello. A Pentium III does not beep. It just sits there, being fast and boring.

Also, I understand the 486. I know which jumper does what. I know that the cache chip gets hot. I know the exact sequence of swear words required to make the sound card work. That is a relationship.

I am not against new computers. I am against computers that do not make any noise.

-- pants
