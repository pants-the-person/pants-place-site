Winamp is the best program on this computer. I will not be taking questions.

The default skin is fine, but the real joy is downloading skins from the internet and making your music player look like a spaceship, a car dashboard, or a piece of cheese. I have 43 skins. I use the one that looks like brushed metal. The others are there for emotional support.

The playlist is the real killer feature. You can queue up 100 MIDI files and let it run all night. The little spectrum analyzer jumps around and makes your computer look like it is doing something important.

My current playlist:

1. Pants Theme (my own composition)
2. Dial-Up Blues
3. 486 Overdrive
4. The song from the shareware game I cannot remember the name of

If you have a skin that looks like a 486 motherboard, email me. I will trade you a MIDI file.
