The modem connects at 52,000 bps on a good day. On a bad day it connects at 33.6 and I have to pretend that is fine.

There is a whole ritual to it. You pick up the phone, you hear the dial tone, you type `ATDT` and a phone number, and then the modem screams at you for thirty seconds. It sounds like robots arguing. Then, silence. Then the little bubbles in the taskbar tell you that you are on the information superhighway.

Loading a page with a big image takes long enough that you can go make a sandwich. You learn to appreciate text. You learn to press Stop before the last three images finish, because you already know what they look like.

Everyone complains about how slow it is. But there is something nice about a connection that you have to wait for. It makes the internet feel like a place you visit instead of a thing that is always there, chewing on your attention.

Anyway, my mom picked up the phone in the middle of a download again. I have to go.

-- pants
