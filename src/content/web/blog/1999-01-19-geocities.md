Rules for a perfect personal homepage:

1. Pick a tiled background. It should be a starfield, a cloud, or a texture you made in Paint. Never a solid color. That is for cowards.
2. Add a marquee. It does not matter what it says. Movement is the point.
3. Use at least three fonts. Comic Sans for the guestbook, Times New Roman for the essays, Courier for the MIDI list.
4. Add an "Under Construction" sign. If your site is finished, you are not trying hard enough.
5. Put a hit counter at the bottom. Nobody knows if it is real. That is fine.
6. Join a webring. The web is a ring of friends, not a search engine.
7. Add a MIDI file that plays automatically. Yes, it is annoying. That is the price of culture.

I have broken most of these rules on this site, which is why it is merely good instead of perfect. The new version has a starfield background and a marquee, so I am getting closer.

Next: animated GIFs. I found a spinning email icon that is 12 KB. The page will be unstoppable.
